# LushLook — Project Overview

## Introduction
LushLook is a proposed platform connecting customers with beauty professionals who provide selected beauty services at the customer's home.

## Problem statement
Customers may find it inconvenient to identify suitable professionals, compare services and coordinate appointments. Beauty professionals may need a convenient way to reach potential customers.

## Objectives
- Make it easier to explore home beauty service options.
- Provide a simple appointment-request flow.
- Present service information clearly.
- Create a foundation for professional profiles and booking management.

## Proposed workflow
Customer opens the application → explores services → selects a service → submits preferred appointment details → request is stored → professional/admin reviews and confirms → service is delivered → customer can provide feedback.

## Proposed modules
1. Customer interface
2. Service catalogue
3. Appointment requests
4. Beauty-professional profiles and availability
5. Admin/booking management
6. Future payment and review modules

## Scope note
This repository currently contains a basic frontend and a small Flask/MySQL booking-request example. Authentication, professional verification, payment integration, live availability, maps and review management are future tasks unless separately developed and tested.
